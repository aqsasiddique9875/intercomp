Recommendation Platform
1. Introduction
The AI-Based Internship & Job Recommendation Platform is a smart cross-platform application designed to connect students with companies for internships and job opportunities.
The system helps companies recruit intelligent and skill-verified students using:
AI-based evaluation
Tests and assignments
Ranking systems
Activity tracking
Skill analysis
The platform also helps students improve their careers through:
Technical courses
Interview preparation
Career guidance
Skill assessments
Internship recommendations
Company select students.
By seeing their profile
Based on skill
The application supports:
Android
iOS
Windows
Web platforms
using Flutter technology.
2. Problem Statement
Many students struggle to find internships and jobs according to their skills and interests. Companies also face difficulty finding verified and talented candidates.
Most current systems only rely on resumes and do not properly evaluate:
Practical skills
Problem-solving ability
Learning consistency
Real performance
This project solves these issues using:
AI-based recommendations
Weekly tests and assignments
Student ranking systems
Skill verification methods
Smart recruitment tools
