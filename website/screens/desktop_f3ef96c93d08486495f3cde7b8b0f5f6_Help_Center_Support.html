Common Questions

How does AI matching work?

expand_more

Our LiquidMatch engine analyzes 42 discrete data points from both job descriptions and candidate profiles, prioritizing verified skills and behavioral indicators over simple keyword matching.

What are Verified Trust Scores?

expand_more

Trust Scores are composite ratings derived from previous employer endorsements, completed skill assessments, and profile completeness. High scores guarantee higher visibility in recruiter searches.

Can I pause my recruitment subscription?

expand_more

Yes, you can pause your monthly subscription for up to 90 days. Your job data and candidate pipelines will be safely stored until you're ready to resume hiring.
